# Gen 3 / Hoenn retention audit — 2026-09-03

Scope: National Dex #252–#386.

This audit applies the same retention rules already accepted for Gen 1–2:

- Evaluate NORMAL, SHADOW, PURIFIED, MEGA / Primal, DYNAMAX and GIGANTAMAX independently.
- A release flag is not itself a reason to KEEP.
- Only material data gaps may create HOLD_FOR_NOW.
- PvE does not require Attack IV = 15. 15 Attack is a same-species investment preference; high-overall-IV candidates such as 14/15/15 are not rejected solely for missing 15 Attack.
- Shadow candidates use a wider retention threshold than normal candidates, but Shadow status does not automatically make a weak raid attacker worth keeping.
- PvP Rank <=100 is the main KEEP bucket and 101–250 is the conditional bucket; rankings outside those buckets do not become a family-wide keep reason.

## PvP snapshot review

Gen 3 now uses the fixed 2026-09-01 PvPoke Open / Overall snapshot at commit
`7b96d91fb553780653190ad32de001b5d9086a7f`.

The previous fixed snapshot and the 2026-09-01 snapshot were compared for every mapped Gen 3
NORMAL and SHADOW form in GL, UL and ML. There are **0 crossings** between the retention buckets:

- TOP_100
- 101_250
- OVER_250 / UNRANKED

The regression is encoded in `tests/gen3-pvp-snapshot-audit.test.ts`. Rank numbers may still move
inside a bucket, but the current snapshot refresh does not require a retention-policy change for Gen 3 PvP.

## PvE review

The old Gen 3 importer attached a form-level PvE label to NORMAL and SHADOW and gave every MEGA a
generic PvE treatment. That could incorrectly make one form's value leak into another BattleVariant.

The audit replaces that behavior with exact BattleVariant PvE classification.

Current raid evidence:

- `PVE-ATTACKERS-HOENN-20260903`: current non-Mega raid-attacker list.
- `PVE-MEGA-HOENN-20260903`: current list with Mega Evolutions / Primal Reversion included.

Working tier interpretation:

- S / A+ / A -> CORE_INVESTMENT
- B+ / B -> USABLE_OR_BUDGET
- C -> SPECIAL_USE
- D or not present -> no standalone raid-retention reason

Important corrections include:

- Sceptile: NORMAL SPECIAL_USE; SHADOW USABLE_OR_BUDGET; MEGA CORE_INVESTMENT.
- Blaziken: NORMAL, SHADOW and MEGA remain CORE_INVESTMENT.
- Swampert: NORMAL no longer inherits a major raid reason; SHADOW USABLE_OR_BUDGET; MEGA CORE_INVESTMENT.
- Gardevoir: NORMAL no longer inherits Shadow / Mega raid value; SHADOW and MEGA are CORE_INVESTMENT.
- Aggron: NORMAL no longer gets an inherited raid keep reason; SHADOW and MEGA are USABLE_OR_BUDGET.
- Manectric: NORMAL no longer inherits the other forms; SHADOW SPECIAL_USE; MEGA USABLE_OR_BUDGET.
- Flygon: NORMAL has no standalone raid reason; SHADOW USABLE_OR_BUDGET.
- Crawdaunt: NORMAL no longer has an inherited raid reason; SHADOW SPECIAL_USE.
- Milotic: NORMAL no longer receives the previous form-level raid keep reason.
- Banette: NORMAL no longer inherits Mega value; MEGA CORE_INVESTMENT.
- Absol: NORMAL and SHADOW are USABLE_OR_BUDGET; MEGA CORE_INVESTMENT.
- Glalie: NORMAL no longer inherits Mega value; MEGA USABLE_OR_BUDGET.
- Walrein: NORMAL no longer receives the old form-level raid keep reason; SHADOW SPECIAL_USE.

- Metagross: NORMAL is USABLE_OR_BUDGET; SHADOW and MEGA are CORE_INVESTMENT.
- Latias: NORMAL has no standalone raid keep reason; SHADOW and MEGA are CORE_INVESTMENT.

utility is separate from direct attacker tier. That default is conditional, not an automatic core KEEP.

## Max Battle review

The previous Gen 3 release sets contained no Hoenn Dynamax forms. The audit refreshes the released
Hoenn Max roster through 2026-09-03 and separates release status from investment value.

The audited released Dynamax forms are:

- #252–#281: Ralts, Kirlia
- #282–#311: Gardevoir, Sableye
- #312–#341: Wailmer, Wailord, Trapinch, Vibrava, Flygon
- #342–#371: Feebas, Milotic, Spheal, Sealeo, Walrein
- #372–#386: Beldum, Metang, Metagross, Regirock, Regice, Registeel, Latias, Latios

Role evidence comes from the September 2026 Max attacker, defender and healer tier lists recorded in the
batch research files. A released Max form is not automatically CORE_INVESTMENT.

A separate correctness fix prevents a NORMAL Pokémon from receiving Max retention value merely because
the species has a released Dynamax version. Normal Pokémon cannot substitute for an actual Max
BattleVariant.

## Mega / Primal boundaries

Mega and Primal variants remain separate BattleVariants. Their direct raid value is evaluated independently
from NORMAL and SHADOW.

NORMAL forms that can Mega Evolve / undergo Primal Reversion may remain conditional base candidates, but
the Mega / Primal raid tier is not copied into the NORMAL PvE tier.

Primal Kyogre and Primal Groudon remain labeled as Primal Reversion rather than ordinary Mega forms in
user-visible text.

## Data-gap policy

No secondary missing field is allowed to turn an otherwise resolved family into HOLD_FOR_NOW. The Gen 3
audit continues to reserve TRUE_DATA_PENDING for a missing fact that could materially change the final
KEEP / CONDITIONAL_KEEP / TRANSFER_CANDIDATE result.

## Merge gate

Gen 3 is ready to merge only after all of the following are true:

1. Clean research rebuild passes.
2. Historical published-data integrity passes.
3. Lint, typecheck and tests pass.
4. Static Pages build and artifact verification pass.
5. Formal review checkpoints and the release snapshot are regenerated from the audited database.
6. The regenerated snapshot is re-verified before the PR is merged.
