# Gen 4 / Sinnoh retention audit — 2026-09-03

Scope: National Dex #387–#493.

This audit follows the accepted Gen 1–3 retention rules:

- NORMAL, SHADOW, PURIFIED, MEGA, DYNAMAX and GIGANTAMAX are separate BattleVariants.
- Release status is not itself a KEEP reason.
- PvE Attack IV = 15 is a same-species investment preference, not a hard transfer threshold.
- Shadow retention is intentionally wider than normal retention, but Shadow status alone does not create raid value.
- PvP Rank <=100 is the primary KEEP bucket and 101–250 is the conditional bucket; movement inside a bucket is not treated as a policy change.
- Only a material missing fact may create HOLD_FOR_NOW.

## PvP snapshot review

Gen 4 is audited against the fixed 2026-09-01 PvPoke snapshot at commit
`7b96d91fb553780653190ad32de001b5d9086a7f`.

Every mapped Gen 4 NORMAL and SHADOW form was compared against the previous fixed GL / UL / ML
snapshot. There are **0 crossings** between the retention buckets TOP_100, 101–250 and
OVER_250 / UNRANKED.

The regression is encoded in `tests/gen4-pvp-snapshot-audit.test.ts`.

## Shadow release review

Later Sinnoh evolutions can inherit a released Shadow state from an already released earlier-generation
Shadow base. The audit now derives those states through the formal evolution graph rather than checking
only direct Sinnoh Shadow encounters.

This fixes cross-generation descendants such as Ambipom, Mismagius, Honchkrow, Weavile, Magnezone,
Rhyperior, Tangrowth, Electivire, Magmortar, Gliscor, Mamoswine, Porygon-Z, Gallade, Probopass,
Dusknoir and Froslass.

Direct Shadow encounter evidence remains distinct from evolution-derived release evidence.

## Mega boundary

Released Gen 4 Mega variants are modeled separately from their normal forms. The current audited
released set includes Mega Lopunny, Mega Garchomp, Mega Lucario, Mega Abomasnow and Mega Gallade.

Mega Froslass, Mega Heatran and Mega Darkrai remain modeled but unreleased. Their existence does not
create a current battle keep reason.

## Unreleased final-Sinnoh boundary

The current Sinnoh release reference still marks Phione (#489), Manaphy (#490), and all Arceus (#493)
forms as unavailable in Pokémon GO. They remain unreleased in the model and cannot create current
retention recommendations.
